Getting started

How to use TANIX AI 3.0

Follow these steps each session for the best experience.

1

Join the Telegram channel

Stay on top of announcements and market commentary. Check it before you start trading for the day.

2

Read the money management guide

Decide your risk per trade and daily loss limit before you take a signal — not after.

3

Open the TANIX 3.0 terminal

Press Scan Market. The engine reads live 1-minute candles and returns a pair, direction (BUY or SELL), confidence and indicator scores.

4

Enter at the entry time

Watch the countdown. Place the trade on your broker at the entry time shown and set the expiry to match. Times follow the time zone you chose at login.

5

Wait for the next signal

When the current signal expires, a "New signal available" notice appears. Never chase losses — stick to your limits.
