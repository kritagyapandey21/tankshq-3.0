How stakes are calculated: the first stake is 1% of your starting balance. After a loss the next stake doubles; after a win it returns to 1% of your current balance. No stake is ever more than 50% of your balance. Doubling after losses grows risk very quickly, so a short losing streak can wipe out an account. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
